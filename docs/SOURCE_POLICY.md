# Source policy

DKKB combines external knowledge with personal experience and derived guidance. Provenance must remain visible.

## Source preference

Prefer standards, specifications, official documentation, primary technical sources, original research, authoritative literature, and then high-quality secondary sources when they fit the claim.

Community discussion can support observations about practice. It is not automatic proof of a technical claim.

## Literature

Summarize useful knowledge in original wording and cite the work. Do not copy substantial protected passages, diagrams, tables, or code without permission or a compatible license.

Organize canonical entries by the engineering concept, not by the chapter structure of a book.

## Personal experience

Identify personal observations as personal experience. Record the relevant context and the limit of the conclusion.

## Derived guidance

Derived guidance combines evidence, experience, or reasoning into a recommendation. Make the reasoning visible when the recommendation is not self-evident.

## AI-assisted research

AI can help find, compare, summarize, and draft material. AI output is not a source.

Verify claims against cited material before publication. Never invent or infer a citation only because it appears plausible.
