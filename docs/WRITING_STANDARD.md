# DKKB writing standard

## Purpose

DKKB uses a controlled technical writing style inspired by ASD-STE100. It is adapted for software engineering and long-form knowledge.

DKKB does not claim strict ASD-STE100 compliance.

The goal is precise, restrained, readable technical English.

## Core rules

1. Prefer short, direct sentences.
2. Put one main idea in a sentence when practical.
3. Keep each paragraph focused on one subject.
4. Prefer active voice when the actor matters.
5. Use one term for one concept.
6. Do not change terms only to avoid repetition.
7. Prefer concrete verbs and nouns.
8. Remove words that do not change technical meaning.
9. Define uncommon terms when the reader cannot infer them from context.
10. Keep established software engineering terms when a simpler word would change the meaning.

A descriptive sentence should normally stay near 25 words or fewer. This is a target, not a mechanical limit.

## Human technical voice

DKKB must not read like generic AI-generated or marketing text.

Do not add a broad introduction when the article can start with the concept or problem. Do not add a conclusion only to repeat the article.

Avoid filler, marketing language, fake sophistication, unnecessary metaphors, and generic headings.

## Punctuation

Do not use an em dash.

Use a period when two independent statements are clearer as two sentences. Use a colon for a direct explanation or list.

## Recommendations

Do not state contextual guidance as a universal rule. State important limits and trade-offs near the recommendation.

## Personal observations

First-person writing is allowed in personal experience and playbook entries. Do not convert a personal observation into impersonal certainty.

## AI-assisted writing

AI-assisted text must be edited to this standard before publication. Check the claim, source, structure, terminology, and tone.

The repository validator blocks selected deterministic style violations. Human review remains responsible for meaning and writing quality.

## Reference

The style is informed by [ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English](https://www.asd-ste100.org/). DKKB adapts its principles to software engineering rather than reproducing the specification as a repository rule set.
